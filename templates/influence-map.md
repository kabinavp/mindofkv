## The three stages

- **Information:** they know your name and which team you are in.
- **Insight:** they know what you are working on and what you achieved recently.
- **Interact:** they come to you to exchange ideas and ask for your input. Growth happens here.

## Moves that work

- **To reach Information:** ask for 30 minutes of input on one specific problem. Give enough context that the request makes sense.
- **To reach Insight:** share a document or a recent result and ask for their review.
- **To reach Interact:** follow up and show what you did with their advice. Then offer help on their problems.
