## The 30-minute agenda

| Time | Step | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 min | Set the scene | Say: “It’s six months after launch. The project failed. Why?” |
| 3–10 min | Write alone | Everyone writes their reasons in silence, so loud voices don’t anchor the room. |
| 10–20 min | Share | Go round the room, one reason at a time, until every reason is on the board. |
| 20–27 min | Rank | Score each reason by likelihood and by damage. |
| 27–30 min | Assign | Give each of the top risks an owner and a first action. |

## After the session

- Add the top risks to the launch plan, with their owners.
- Set up monitoring for each early warning sign.
- Review this table a week before launch.
