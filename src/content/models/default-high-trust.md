In fast-growing companies, teams complain about misalignment and poor output. Underneath, the problem is usually a **trust deficit**, and a trust deficit breeds anxiety, politics and slow work.

The model is to start from high trust by default. With stakeholders, that means regular conversations from day one and an agreed way of working, a shared “trust code”. It feels like an uphill climb at first, while both sides adjust, but it becomes the base everything else is built on.

## Signs you need it

- Teams describe each other as “they”.
- Decisions get re-litigated after meetings.
- People cc managers to protect themselves.

## Where to start

Assume good intent, show up consistently, and make your working style explicit, so others can predict you. Trust grows from being predictable as much as from being right.
