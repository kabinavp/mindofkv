When appraisals disappoint, it’s tempting to blame the manager or the organisation. A better move is to ask: **what would it take to get to the next level?** Then write down the answer, share the notes, and report progress against them.

Over time, the conversation with your manager turns into a conversation about your growth.

## The loop

1. **Know the bar.** Use your organisation’s competency map, or define it with your manager if none exists.
2. **Write your own gap analysis,** and be honest about it.
3. **Validate it** with your manager.
4. **Set personal goals** for the quarter or year: deepen expertise, explore a new area, or move into managing people.
5. **Track and share progress** regularly, so nothing is a surprise at review time.
