For years I built things quietly. I'd start a project, work on it in private, and either abandon it or ship it to complete silence. This site is me trying something different.

## The problem with building in private

When nobody's watching, there's no cost to quitting. There's also no feedback, no serendipity, and no one to tell you that the thing you're stuck on is actually a solved problem.

> Shipping is a skill. Sharing is a multiplier.

## What I'll share

- What I'm building, with real numbers
- What I learn along the way
- The mistakes, especially the expensive ones
