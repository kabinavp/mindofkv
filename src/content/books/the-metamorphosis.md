Gregor Samsa, a travelling salesman who supports his whole family, wakes up one morning transformed into a monstrous insect. What follows is less about the transformation than about how his family reacts to it: duty turning into resentment, love into relief. A strange, funny, devastating short book about work, worth and belonging.
