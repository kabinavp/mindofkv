The most useful hundred pages I’ve read on talking to users. The core idea is simple: people lie to be nice, so stop asking whether they’d use your idea and ask about their life instead — what they did last time, what it cost them, what they’ve already tried. I reread it before every new project, and it has saved me from building at least two things nobody wanted.
