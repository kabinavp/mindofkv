A short, aching novella. A lonely young dreamer wandering St Petersburg during the summer “white nights” meets Nastenka on a canal bank, and over four nights they share their lives while she waits for a lover who promised to return. It’s Dostoevsky at his most tender, and you can read it in one sitting.
