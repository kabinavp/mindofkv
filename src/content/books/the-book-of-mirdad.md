A mystical allegory by the Lebanese writer Mikhail Naimy. A mysterious figure named Mirdad arrives at a mountaintop monastery called the Ark and gradually reveals himself as a teacher, speaking with the companions about love, silence, prayer and freedom. Part parable and part scripture, it rewards slow reading.
