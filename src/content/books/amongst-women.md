Michael Moran, a former IRA fighter, rules his family on a farm in the west of Ireland long after the war that defined him has ended. His wife and daughters orbit him with love and fear, and his sons drift away. A quiet, precise novel about power inside a family, shortlisted for the Booker Prize.
