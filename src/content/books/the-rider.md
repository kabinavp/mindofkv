One amateur road race, 150 kilometres, told in real time from inside the rider’s head. It’s short, funny and brutal, and it captures exactly what a hard climb feels like: the bargaining, the suffering, the absurd pride. Anyone who rides will recognise themselves on every page, and anyone who doesn’t will finally understand why we do it.
