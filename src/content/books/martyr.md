The debut novel from the poet Kaveh Akbar. Cyrus Shams, a young Iranian-American poet in Indiana, newly sober and adrift, becomes obsessed with the idea of a death that means something. His mother died when Iran Air Flight 655 was shot down in 1988, and his search for answers leads him to a dying artist whose final exhibition is at the Brooklyn Museum.
