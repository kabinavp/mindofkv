---
stance: Small teams don’t need OKRs.
topic: Teams
since: 2025-02-14
model: momentum-over-measurement
order: 1
---
For a small team, speed is the only real advantage, and quarterly goal-setting eats into it. A weekly rhythm works better: plan on Monday, ship through the week, review on Friday. When execution slows, it’s rarely because OKRs are missing.
